Privacy Policy

Orbit Tycoon  ·  Last updated: August 16, 2026

Overview

Orbit Tycoon is a mobile game developed by Tinker Apps. Tinker Apps does not operate its own backend servers and does not intentionally store personal data on infrastructure controlled by Tinker Apps. However, certain third-party services integrated into the app (such as Google services) process data on our behalf or as independent data controllers, as described in this policy. Tinker Apps does not sell personal data. All Google services referenced in this policy operate under Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Controller

The data controller responsible for this app is Tinker Apps (United States). You can contact us at tinkerapps.support@gmail.com.

Data We Collect

Tinker Apps does not operate its own backend servers and does not intentionally store personal data on infrastructure it controls. Game progress — including credits, upgrades, inventory, and settings — is stored locally on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, this progress data is synced to your Google account. Game save data is designed not to include directly identifying personal information.

Google AdMob

Orbit Tycoon displays ads served by Google AdMob, including interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown at natural breaks in play) and rewarded ads (optional ads you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward). When an ad is served, AdMob may independently collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data

This data is collected by AdMob and shared with Google and its advertising partners for ad targeting and measurement. AdMob processes this data according to Google's privacy practices and policies; Tinker Apps does not independently access or use this advertising data outside of the reports and controls Google provides. You can opt out of personalised advertising and limit certain ad-related data collection in your device settings under Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalisation. AdMob is operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. For details of how Google uses information — including IP addresses — from sites and apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads in the EEA, UK & Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Orbit Tycoon uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a consent message before any personalised advertising. Depending on the choice you make:

  • If you consent, AdMob may serve personalised ads and process data as described in the section above.
  • If you do not consent, ads are served as non-personalised — they are not targeted based on behavioural profiling or personalised advertising profiles. Google may still process limited contextual, security, fraud-prevention, or operational data necessary to deliver ads in accordance with applicable law and Google's own policies.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from within the app under Settings > Manage Ad Privacy Options, which re-opens the consent form. Where Google Play indicates that a user is a minor, ads are served as non-personalised regardless of consent.

Google Play Billing & In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. Tinker Apps does not receive or store any payment information. All purchase validation is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Orbit Tycoon offers the following types of in-app purchases:

  • Token bundles (100 / 400 / 1,000 tokens) — consumable, one-time grants. Tokens cannot be restored in the event of data loss, device change, or account transfer.
  • No Ads Pack — includes a one-time grant of 50 tokens (non-restorable) plus permanent benefits: ad removal and 2× material haul. The permanent benefits are non-consumable and linked to your Google Play account; they restore automatically on reinstall.
  • Starter Pack — includes a one-time grant of 100 tokens (non-restorable) plus 2 permanent bonus fleet slots. The fleet slots are non-consumable and linked to your Google Play account; they restore automatically on reinstall.

Token grants (including those bundled with packs) are not recoverable if game data is deleted or lost. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, only non-consumable benefits will be restored automatically via your Google Play account.

Google Play Games Services

The app optionally integrates with Google Play Games Services for sign-in, cloud saves, and achievement tracking. If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, Play Games profile data is not shared. However, other integrated Google services (such as AdMob and Firebase) may still process data as described elsewhere in this policy.

If you do sign in, Google Play Games Services independently collects the following for app functionality purposes:

  • User ID (Gamer ID) — a unique identifier linking your Google account to your in-game profile
  • App activity — achievement progress and cloud save data used to power Google Play Games features

Orbit Tycoon uses Google Play Games Services solely to provide sign-in, cloud saves, and achievements. Tinker Apps does not store this information on its own servers or use it for any other purpose. Google Play Games Services operates as an independent data controller under Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Analytics

Orbit Tycoon uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game. Some of this is collected automatically by the Firebase SDK; the game also deliberately records events of its own, listed below. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App events — for example: opening the app and starting a session, which screen you are on (orbit view, market, contracts, settings), deploying satellites, buying upgrades (and when you tried to and could not afford it), accepting and completing contracts, spending premium tokens and what you spent them on, running a Kessler Protocol prestige, tutorial progress, rewarded and full-screen ad placements and whether an ad was watched, each step of an in-app purchase (starting checkout, completing it, or the reason one did not complete), and whether you are signed in to Google Play Games and whether a cloud save was restored
  • Purchase events, including the amount and currency of an in-app purchase you complete, so that revenue can be reported alongside gameplay. Tinker Apps never receives, sees, or stores your payment details — see Google Play Billing below.
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • A randomly-generated app instance ID
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

Player segments. Alongside individual events, Orbit Tycoon records a small set of coarse labels describing what kind of player you are, so that the reports above can be broken down by group rather than read as one undifferentiated total. For Orbit Tycoon these are: whether you have bought the No Ads Pack or the Starter Pack, whether you have made any purchase at all, which orbit stage you have reached, how many prestige runs you have completed, roughly how large your fleet is, roughly how much debris you have captured, whether you finished the tutorial, and whether you are signed in to Google Play Games. These labels are deliberately coarse ranges rather than exact values (for example "10–29 captures", not "seventeen captures"), they describe your activity in this game only, and they are not used to target advertising.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Tinker Apps uses this data in aggregate only to improve gameplay and does not use it to directly identify individual users. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Firebase Analytics does not store or use device identifiers until you consent through Google's consent message; if consent is not given, analytics identifiers remain disabled (Analytics may still record limited, non-identifying information needed for basic operation). You may also be able to limit certain data collection through your device or Google privacy settings.

Firebase Crashlytics

Orbit Tycoon uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect and report app crashes and errors. When a crash occurs, Crashlytics may collect:

  • Stack traces and crash logs
  • Device state at the time of the crash (device model, OS version, available memory)
  • A randomly-generated installation UUID

This data is used solely to diagnose and fix bugs and is not used to identify individual users. Crashlytics is operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Age Signals & Age-Appropriate Experiences

To support applicable U.S. state app-store age-verification laws (such as the Texas App Store Accountability Act, SB 2420), Orbit Tycoon uses the Google Play Age Signals API. Where required by law in your region, Google Play may verify a user's age and provide the app with an age category (for example, an age range such as 0–12, 13–15, 16–17, or 18+) and parental-consent or supervision status.

Orbit Tycoon uses this information only to provide an age-appropriate experience — specifically, to apply more restrictive, non-personalised advertising settings for users who are minors — and for legal compliance and safety, as permitted by these laws. In-app purchases are not restricted by the app based on this signal; purchases by minors are gated by Google Play's own parental-approval controls (such as Family Link / "Ask to Buy"). Tinker Apps does not use this age information for any other purpose and does not store it on its own servers. Age verification and parental consent are handled by Google Play. This signal is provided only for users in regions where it is legally required; in all other cases the app applies its standard settings.

Data Retention

Tinker Apps does not retain personal data on its own servers. Retention periods for data processed by Google services are governed by Google's own policies:

  • AdMob — ad interaction and identifier data is retained per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics — event-level data is retained for a limited period configured within Firebase (subject to Google's defaults, which may change from time to time); aggregate data may be retained for longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports are retained for a limited period set by Google.
  • Google Play Games Services — cloud save and achievement data is retained until you delete it via your Google account.

Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data in your device settings.

Data Deletion

Tinker Apps does not store your data on its own infrastructure. To manage or remove data:

  • Local game data — uninstall the app or clear app data in your device settings.
  • Cloud save and achievement data — delete via your Google Play Games profile or Google Account settings under Settings > Delete Play Games account and data.
  • AdMob data — managed by Google; visit myaccount.google.com.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics data — processed through Google services and may not always be associated with your Google account. Retention and deletion are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Some data collected by third-party services (such as Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) may not be individually identifiable or deletable, but is used in aggregate and retained according to Google's data retention policies.
